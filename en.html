About the Team

Peso Pesado Team was founded in 2000 by Elcio Vicente Piccolo Jr., "Bolado", a 5th-degree black belt promoted by Jorge Patino "Macaco". Over the years, the team has produced more than 50 black belts and taken several athletes to the world's biggest MMA event, the UFC.
