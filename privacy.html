Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Peso Pesado Team handles the personal data collected through this website, in accordance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, Law No. 13,709/2018 — LGPD).

1. Who the data controller is

The controller of the data processed on this website is Peso Pesado Team, a martial arts academy located at R. Dona Cesária Fagundes, 117 — Vila da Saúde, São Paulo - SP, 04054-030, Brazil.

2. What data we collect

We only collect the data you provide yourself through the "Book a Free Class" form:

  • Name
  • Phone
  • E-mail

This website does not use its own cookies, has no user login and does not store this data in any database or server of ours. After the anti-bot check, your browser keeps only a temporary note that it was completed, which is erased when you close the tab.

3. How we use your data

When you fill in and submit the form, your data is used only to compose a message that opens directly in WhatsApp, so you can send us your trial class request. Sending it requires your confirmation within WhatsApp itself. From then on, the conversation and the data you provided exist in Peso Pesado Team's WhatsApp Business account, under the WhatsApp/Meta privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal basis for processing

This data is processed based on the data subject's consent, given voluntarily by filling in the form, and on our legitimate interest in responding to a business contact requested by the data subject (article 7, items I and IX, of the LGPD).

5. Sharing with third parties

This website uses the following third-party services, which may process data under their own policies:

  • WhatsApp (Meta) — where the booking form is sent.
  • Google Maps — map embedded in the "Location" section, which may collect browsing data under Google's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — anti-bot check shown before accessing our contact options. To confirm the visitor is human, it may collect technical data about the browser and device, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

6. Images of students and instructors

Class and instructor photos shown on this website are used with the prior authorization of the students or, in the case of minors, of their parents or guardians, obtained upon enrollment at the academy.

7. Your rights as a data subject

Under the LGPD, you may request at any time: confirmation that your data is being processed, access to the data, correction of incomplete or outdated data, deletion of data processed based on your consent, and withdrawal of the consent given.

To exercise these rights, contact us on WhatsApp at (11) •••••-••••.

8. Security

This website is static (it has no database or application server of its own), which reduces the risk of data leaks through the website being hacked. Communication with the website takes place over a secure connection (HTTPS).

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.
